I keep paper notebooks for everything I build. They're full of crossed-out dimensions, half-working circuits and sketches that only make sense to me. This site is the same idea, but with a bit more clarity. Consider it a write-up and my attempt at improving my documentation skills.

## What goes in here

- Builds in progress, including the ones that don't work
- Notes on Hack The Box machines, written without spoilers
- Notes on anything I find cool
- Projects I build for class
- And more recently, things I learn about cameras, film and the chemistry in between

## How I write

Short entries, written while the details are still fresh. If something failed, the entry says so, because the failures are where most of the learning is.
