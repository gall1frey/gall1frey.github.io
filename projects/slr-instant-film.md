# 2026-10-12 | Fitting a mirror and a film pack in one body

An SLR and an instant camera each take up a lot of room behind the lens, and they want the same room.

## What the SLR needs

- A lens at the right distance from the film plane
- A mirror at 45 degrees that flips out of the way when you shoot
- A focusing screen and a pentaprism so the image comes out the right way up
- A shutter between the mirror and the film

## What instant film needs

- A flat film plane exactly where the image comes into focus
- Space for a film pack behind or beside that plane
- A path and rollers so the photo can leave the camera while the chemistry spreads evenly

> The Polaroid SX-70 is the well-known answer to this puzzle. It folds flat and hides its mirror assembly inside a clever body.

I'm studying how that design solves it before I commit to my own layout. The exit path for the photo is still undecided.
